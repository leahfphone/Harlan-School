Printed page 33

Natural Features

Many rivers have their headwaters in the Rocky Mountains, some of which flow east across the plains and some of which flow west toward the Pacific Ocean. Locate the map on pp. 346–347 in the student text NWHG. Find the following rivers on the map and trace their routes from their beginnings in the Rockies to their destinations east or west.

The West is known for its rugged mountains. The majestic chain of the Rocky Mountains is called the “Roof of America.” Farther west are two other mountain chains.

Between the western mountains are plateaus, valleys, and a vast arid region called the North American Desert. This desert covers parts of eleven western states and is divided into five regions. Study the map and locate the five regions.
