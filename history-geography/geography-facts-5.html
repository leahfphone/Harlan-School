Geography Facts 5 · Bodies of Water and Islands

Textbook pages 364–365

Four Oceans

Three of the world’s four oceans border the Western Hemisphere: the Pacific Ocean, Atlantic Ocean, and Arctic Ocean. Note the four oceans on the globes to the right.

The Ocean That Is Not an Ocean

The three largest oceans come together around the frozen South Pole continent. For many years, geographers did not know that Antarctica is a continent. Until brave explorers studied the area and found land beneath the ice, it was thought to be a frozen ocean like the Arctic. It was called the Antarctic Ocean. Today, the parts of the Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian Oceans closest to Antarctica are sometimes called the Antarctic Ocean.

Islands

An island is a landform that is completely surrounded by water.
