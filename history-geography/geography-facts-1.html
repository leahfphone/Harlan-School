Geography Facts 1 · Hemispheres and Continents

Textbook page 356 · Map Mastery 1 on page 357

As you look at a globe, you can see only half of the sphere at one time. Half a sphere is called a hemisphere.

There are two ways to divide the earth into hemispheres. One way is to divide it at the equator. The area between the equator and the North Pole is called the Northern Hemisphere, or the northern half of the earth. The southern half of the earth, between the equator and the South Pole, is called the Southern Hemisphere.

The Northern Hemisphere contains most of the world’s land masses; the Southern Hemisphere contains most of the world’s water.

The other way to divide the earth into hemispheres is to divide it between east and west. The Eastern Hemisphere has more land, and the Western Hemisphere has more water.

The earth’s largest land areas are called continents. The Western Hemisphere has two continents: North America and South America.

All the inhabitants of the Western Hemisphere are descendants of people from the Eastern Hemisphere. This is why the Eastern Hemisphere is often called the Old World and the Western Hemisphere is called the New World.
