Using Latitude and Longitude

When the lines of latitude and the lines of longitude are shown together on a map, the resulting “squaring off” of the map is called a grid. By using the numbers on the grid, we can pinpoint any location on earth.

On the world map below, find the two lines of latitude numbered 60°. Which is 60° north latitude? Find 20° south latitude. What line is 0° latitude? Now find 0° longitude. What is it called? What longitude makes up most of the International Date Line? The point 60° north latitude, 80° west longitude goes through what bay? What continent touches 40° north latitude, 80° east longitude?
