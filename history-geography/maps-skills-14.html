Plains and Hills

In general, the area between the Appalachian Mountains and the Rocky Mountains consists of either flat plains or gently rolling hills. There are a few low mountain ranges, however. Locate the map on pp. 346–347 in the student text NWHG. In what states do you find the Black Hills? Ozark Mountains? Mesabi Range? Can you find the Great Plains?

America is unusual because her midwest, though far from the sea, is still heavily populated. Business in the Midwest flourishes because of two routes to the sea: the St. Lawrence Seaway and the Mississippi River.

Natural Boundaries

Find the Straits of Mackinac, an important link in the water route between Lake Michigan and the Atlantic Ocean. The straits connect Lake Michigan and Lake Huron at the northern end of the Lower Peninsula of Michigan.
