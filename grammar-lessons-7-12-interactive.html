Correct each run-on using a comma and conjunction, a semicolon, or separate sentences.

9a1. Correct this run-on: Mr. Parker enjoyed the concert, he wished his seat had been nearer the front.
9a2. Correct this run-on: I expected the examination to be challenging it was.

Lesson 9: Combining Related Ideas

Combine each pair into one sentence using although, because, if, since, or unless.

9w1. Combine into one sentence using although, because, if, since, or unless: I work out every day. I may pass the physical fitness test.
9w2. Combine into one sentence using although, because, if, since, or unless: I worked out for two months. I passed the physical fitness test.
9w3. Combine into one sentence using although, because, if, since, or unless: I worked out for two months. I failed the physical fitness test.

Lesson 10: Run-Together Sentences — Method One

Delete unnecessary “and so” or “and then.” Add capitals and end marks to make good sentences.

10a1. Rewrite this run-together sentence using Method One: Charles A. Lindbergh was the first person to fly alone across the Atlantic and so he received a hero’s welcome back to America.
10a2. Rewrite this run-together sentence using Method One: The radio became popular in American homes and then “Amos ’n’ Andy” was a favorite comic radio show.
10a3. Rewrite this run-together sentence using Method One: Early automobiles replaced the horse and so many stalled motorists wished to have their horses back.

Lesson 11: Run-Together Sentences — Method Two

Use because, when, or after to show the relationship between ideas.

11b1. Rewrite using Method Two. Begin with because, when, or after: Charles A. Lindbergh was the first person to fly alone across the Atlantic and so he received a hero’s welcome back to America.
11b2. Rewrite using Method Two. Begin with because, when, or after: The radio became popular in American homes and then “Amos ’n’ Andy” was a favorite comic radio show.
11b3. Rewrite using Method Two. Begin with because, when, or after: Early automobiles replaced the horse and so many stalled motorists wished to have their horses back.
