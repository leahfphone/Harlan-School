Starter Spelling & Vocabulary Practice

This is a starter set, not an assigned spelling list. Practice five words at your own pace. There is no timer or grade.

How to practice

First, study a word and its meaning. Then hide the word, listen, and try spelling it. Choose its meaning and check your answers. You can try again as often as you need.

Use Listen to hear a word. If audio is unavailable, use the study word and example on screen.

Starter word list

Use this list for study or paper practice.

  • observe — to watch carefully. Observe the bird as it builds a nest.
  • compare — to look for ways things are alike or different. Compare the two maps.
  • predict — to say what you think will happen. Predict how the story will end.
  • evidence — facts or details that support an idea. Footprints are evidence that someone walked here.
  • journey — a trip from one place to another. Our journey took us across the mountains.
