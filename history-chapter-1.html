1.1 The Greatest Migration in History

History is the record of what has happened to people. Geography is the study of Earth and its lands, waters, people, and environments. North America, South America, and nearby islands are part of the Western Hemisphere.

The textbook describes early migrations after the Tower of Babel. A migration is the movement of people or animals from one region to another. One proposed route from Asia to North America was across land in the area of today's Bering Strait, between Russia and Alaska. A strait is a narrow waterway joining two larger bodies of water.

Animals and people moved into different regions. A habitat is a place with conditions suitable for particular plants and animals.

1.2 The Land the Americans Settled

Different communities settled throughout North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and South America. Each developed its own culture, or way of life.

North America includes the Rocky Mountains, Appalachian Mountains, Great Plains, Hudson Bay, Gulf of Mexico, Mississippi River, and Greenland. The Isthmus of Panama links North and South America. Mexico was home to the Aztecs; the Maya lived in Central America.

The West Indies are Caribbean islands. South America includes the Andes Mountains, Amazon River, and Atacama Desert. The Inca built an important civilization in the Andes. Cape Horn lies near South America's southern end.

1.3 How the American Indians Lived

Indigenous peoples developed many different traditions, languages, skills, and ways of life. Children learned skills from their families and communities. The Maya and Aztecs also developed formal education systems.

Foods included hunted animals, fish, maize, beans, squash, nuts, and berries. Homes varied by region and included tipis, wigwams, hogans, and other buildings. Recreation included running, archery, ball games, and lacrosse. Indigenous communities held diverse religious beliefs and practices. The textbook discusses these from its Christian perspective.

Jim Thorpe was a celebrated athlete. Will Rogers was a famous storyteller and entertainer.
